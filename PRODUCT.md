## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the client brief: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion, and Lucide icons for this greenfield project.

## Users

Businesses, teams, event organisers, institutions, and brand owners in India who are evaluating custom apparel, merchandise, and corporate gifts and want a quick, human quotation rather than an ecommerce checkout.

## Product Purpose

Texa Mart's website makes its merchandise catalogue easy to explore and turns product interest into a qualified WhatsApp enquiry. Success means a visitor can understand the offer, find a suitable product, review confirmed price options and MOQ information, and start a useful quotation conversation.

## Positioning

Texa Mart presents custom merchandise as a premium brand medium: things people can wear, carry, use, and remember, with quotation and order details completed directly through WhatsApp.

TEXA T-Shirts is a Mysuru-based custom apparel and garment brand serving businesses, institutions, schools, events, teams, brands and individuals. Its confirmed apparel offer includes custom and polo T-shirts, corporate and promotional apparel, school and institutional apparel, sports and event T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, caps, printed and embroidered apparel, and customized or bulk garment orders.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse by category or product, review pricing and minimum quantities where supplied, select product or gift-set options, then send a preformatted enquiry to Texa Mart on WhatsApp. The site is a catalogue and lead-generation experience, not an ecommerce store.

- Positioning line: Custom Printing • Merchandise • Corporate Gifts.
- Preserve the supplied Texa Mart logo without redesigning, distorting, or recolouring it.

## Product Principles

1. Make merchandise desirable before making the catalogue exhaustive.
2. Keep the route from product discovery to a useful WhatsApp enquiry unmistakable.
3. Present every price, MOQ, and claim with strict fidelity to supplied material.
4. Design mobile as an intentional browsing and enquiry experience.
5. Let the product imagery and Texa Mart identity carry the visual story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, clear focus states, AA contrast, labelled forms, accessible menu and lightbox behaviours, sufficiently large touch targets, and complete reduced-motion fallbacks.
